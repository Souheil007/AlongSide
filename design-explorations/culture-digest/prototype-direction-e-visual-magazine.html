Why It Feels Like They Started Speaking Another Language Overnight

Between the ages of 13 and 15, the human brain undergoes an aggressive wave of synaptic pruning in the prefrontal cortex while the limbic reward system surges with dopamine sensitivity. The result is an intense, evolutionary urgency to belong to a peer tribe rather than the domestic family unit.

Words like "Aura points" and "Crashout" are not random nonsense or brain-rot; they are rapid-fire emotional shorthand designed to establish peer hierarchy without parental surveillance. When Julian talks about gaining or losing Aura, he is actively mapping social risk and psychological safety.

"If you mock their slang, you confirm their fear that you belong to an irrelevant past. If you copy their slang, you induce cringe. The only superpower is calm, playful curiosity."
Artifact 01 · Linguistic Anatomy Velocity: Surging 🔥

Crashout

/ˈkræʃ.aʊt/ · verb/noun

To recklessly explode in anger, abandon emotional regulation, or blow up a social situation without regard for consequences.
