Word / Phenomenon of the Week

"Peek-a-Boo & The Vanishing Act"

Cultural Origin: Universal Infant Play

Parent Calibration Level: 1/5 Completely Safe & Encouraged

Universal infant bonding exercise. Absolutely zero cringe for parents—this is optimal developmental sparring.

Parent Pitfall (Avoid):

Interrupting their focus with sudden toys or rushing the turn-taking moment.

Parent Calm Posture (Try This):

Cover your face with a light cloth, wait 3 seconds for them to reach out, then smile and say "BOO!" softly.
