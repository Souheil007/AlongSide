Surging This Week

"Crashout"

/ˈkræʃ.aʊt/

Verb/Noun: An uncontrollable angry outburst or reckless overreaction to perceived social slights, often triggered by chronic peer fatigue.

Natural Peer Cadence & Context

"Example: 'I was trying to stay chill but after the third test question I literally crashed out.'"

Parental Usage Protocol: ⚠️ DO NOT USE (100% Cringe)

If a parent uses this word out loud, it immediately triggers peer-distancing. Understand it when heard, but respond with calm adult vocabulary: "Sounds like your nervous system hit a redline."
