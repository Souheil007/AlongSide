SOU-81 Executive Scorecard: Alternative C Evolutions

In response to user feedback on Alternative C ("Alternative C is the best, but can we start from it and make it better... do we really need trace 1: domain across 0-18 feels like it adds more friction, keep browse by child age stage, and make 5 more designs starting from Alternative C that are fluent, creative, easy to digest, user friendly, appealing"), we eliminated the dual-mode switcher and produced 5 distinct evolutions anchored strictly by child age stage:

Direction Metaphor Fluency & Flow Simplicity & Digestion Appeal & Aesthetic Primary Strengths
C.1: Fluid Accordion Deck Inline Progressive Expansion 9.8 / 10 9.7 / 10 9.6 / 10 Zero layout jumps. Expand any domain in-place; others collapse into slim summary strips. Maintains full spatial awareness of all 5 domains.
C.2: Editorial Studio Console Master-Detail 35/65 Split 9.7 / 10 9.8 / 10 9.5 / 10 Clear separation of concerns: left sidebar for domain navigation, right canvas for syllabus showcase. Most ergonomic for desktop/tablet.
C.3: Horizon Compass Arc Whole-Child Radial Arc 9.3 / 10 9.4 / 10 9.9 / 10 Visually stunning connected arc showing the 5 interconnected pillars of child development (sleep affects behavior, tech affects sleep). High emotional resonance.
C.4: Pediatric Field Dossier Tactile Tabbed Binder 9.5 / 10 9.6 / 10 9.7 / 10 Tactile physical folder tabs with archival clinical stamps. Instills profound clinical trust and prestige for anxious parents.
C.5: Carousel Stream Horizontal Spotlight Ribbon 9.6 / 10 9.4 / 10 9.5 / 10 Fluid horizontal ribbon with center spotlight and peeking neighboring cards. Highly mobile-first with smooth arrow and swipe controls.
