Tonight's Scenario:

Maya arrives home furious, slams the kitchen cabinet door, and screams: “No one at school likes me!”

How Will You Respond?
Path A: The Logical Fix (Reactive) Common Default

“Don't slam cabinets in this house! Why are you always making drama? You have plenty of friends.”

Path B: The Empathetic Bridge (Recommended) Calm Co-Regulation

“Oof, that looks like a really heavy day. You don't have to talk right now, but I made tea and I'm right here.”

Why Path B Prevents an Evening Blowup:

Adolescents don't slam doors because they hate cabinets—they slam doors because their emotional cup is spilling over. Offering silent physical safety invites them to open up when cortisol drops.
