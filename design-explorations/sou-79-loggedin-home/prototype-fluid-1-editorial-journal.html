When Screen Time Ends: Shifting from Defiance to Co-Regulation

Spoken Audio Briefing Dr. Aliza Pressman

Duration: 3 minutes 45 seconds

When a child screams during screen transition, their prefrontal cortex has just experienced an abrupt dopamine drop. Treating this as moral defiance triggers defensive defiance. Instead, step into their visual field, offer physical co-presence 2 minutes before the transition, and bridge into a tactile activity.
