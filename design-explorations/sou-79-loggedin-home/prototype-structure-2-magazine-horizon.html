Lesson Takeaway (3 Min Read)

When an 8-year-old enters a high-stimulus game, their prefrontal cortex locks into hyper-focus. Demanding immediate compliance activates fight-or-flight defensiveness. Instead, enter their physical orbit 3 minutes beforehand and use co-regulation bridging.

Script for Tonight
