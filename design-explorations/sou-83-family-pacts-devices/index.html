Definitive Recommendation: Direction A (The Calm Pact & Curfew Cockpit) with Selected Direction B Enhancements

Direction A is unequivocally the strongest overall design direction. It honors the "Parent Calm" promise by answering the parent's core emotional question ("Is bedtime peace protected tonight?") in under 2 seconds without cognitive burden or tab switching.

Recommended Hybrid Synthesis:
  • Use Direction A's layout and information architecture: Single unified cockpit, zero tab flipping, hero Curfew Dock, side-by-side mutual commitments card, and clean drawer for bridge ladder.
  • Incorporate Direction B's interactive phone screen preview inside the QR Enrollment modal so parents can see the calm lock screen state before scanning.
  • Include Direction B's dual verified signature badges on the family pact card to celebrate bilateral collaboration.
Current Production Diagnostic

UX Audit & Cognitive Friction Analysis

Summary of findings from `PAGE_AUDIT.md`

1. 4-Tab Cognitive Fragmentation

Splits digital parenting into 4 artificial silos: Thinking About Device, Pact, Curfews, Slang. In reality, parents see rules and enforcement as two sides of the same coin.

2. Enterprise MDM Jargon Overload

Terms like "PKCS#7 cryptographic signing", "tamper webhook", and "configuration profile" create anxiety that AlongSide is invasive spyware rather than a calm sleep protector.

3. Severe Redundancy with `/digest`

Tab 4 duplicates the entire Sunday Culture Digest (slang decoders, exploit alerts, dinner starter) which already has its own dedicated top-level route in the navigation bar.

4. Obscured Primary User Action

Confirming tonight's 8:30 PM bedtime lock is buried under secondary table cards and outline buttons rather than presented as a reassuring operational hero.

Architecture Blueprint

Three Genuinely Distinct UX Approaches

Summary of specifications from `DESIGN_ALTERNATIVES.md`

Direction A: Minimal & Focused ("The Calm Cockpit") Recommended

Collapses tabs into a single unified cockpit. Hero Curfew Status Dock + Side-by-side Mutual Commitments card. Secondary tools in clean drawers. 1-click +30 min pass. Time to value: < 2 seconds.

Direction B: Visual & Interactive ("The Living Treaty & Device Dock")

Show, don't tell. Interactive 24-hour bedtime arc timeline with slider, living parchment treaty with dual signature wax seals, and live smartphone simulator showing the calm bedtime screen.

Direction C: Structured & Guided ("The 3-Stage Journey")

Stepwise progression for parents who feel overwhelmed: Stage 1 (Readiness Diagnostic) → Stage 2 (Bilateral Agreement) → Stage 3 (Automated Curfew). Expandable accordions with calm coaching scripts.
