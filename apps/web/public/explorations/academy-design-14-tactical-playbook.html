DRILL 01 // DIGITAL CRASH ACUTE: LEVEL 1

The 60-Second Dopamine Decompression

Target situation: 8-year-old child screaming, crying, or throwing controller after being asked to turn off Minecraft.

01
Silence for 90 Seconds: Do not ask questions or demand immediate apologies. Striatal dopamine is bottoming out.
02
Kinetic Sensory Bridge: Hand child cold ice water or crunchy apple slices. Physical chewing engages parasympathetic nerve.
03
Curiosity Over Disciplinary Enforcement: “Show me what you were building before you close the lid.”

DRILL 02 // SIBLING BATTLE ACUTE: LEVEL 1

The Sibling Territory Partition Call

Target situation: 8-year-old shoving 5-year-old who entered their room or touched their Lego build without consent.

01
Physical Partition First: Place your body calmly between the children. No hands grabbing either child.
02
Acknowledge Age-8 Dignity: “You worked for an hour on this tower. It makes sense you felt panicky when it was touched.”
03
Neutral Limit Without Shaming: “I will protect your build, and I will protect your brother. Hitting is not an option here.”
