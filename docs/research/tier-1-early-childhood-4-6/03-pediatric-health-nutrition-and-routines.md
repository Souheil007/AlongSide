## 1. Pediatric Nutrition Science & Fueling the Active Mind

### 1.1 Caloric Demands & Macronutrient Balancing
Children ages 4 to 6 require approximately **1,200 to 1,600 kcal per day**, depending on physical activity:
- **Protein (19 g/day):** Supports continuous muscular remodeling and immune synthesis (eggs, poultry, fish, Greek yogurt, lentils, tofu).
- **Dietary Fiber (17–20 g/day):** Critical for microbiome diversity and preventing pediatric constipation; berries, apples with skin, oats, chia seeds, broccoli.
- **Healthy Fats (30–35% of total calories):** Continued myelination of the corpus callosum and cortical networks requires omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids (EPA/DHA from wild salmon, sardines, walnuts).

### 1.2 School Lunchbox Engineering & Blood Sugar Stabilization
- **The Midday Sugar Crash Hazard:** Lunches dominated by refined simple sugars (juice boxes, fruit gummies, white bread with jam) cause rapid glycemic spikes followed by precipitous 1:30 PM blood sugar drops. Teachers mistake this neuroglycopenia for ADHD, hyperactivity, or sudden defiance.
- **The Sturdy Lunchbox Blueprint:**
  1. *Clean Protein Anchor:* Hardboiled egg, rolled turkey slices, or edamame.
  2. *Complex Fiber Carb:* Whole wheat pita triangles with hummus or seeded crackers.
  3. *Raw Crunch / Vegetable:* Cucumber rounds, bell pepper sticks, cherry tomatoes (quartered).
  4. *Low-Glycemic Fruit:* Blueberries, raspberries, or sliced kiwi.
  5. *Hydration:* Cold water in a vacuum-insulated stainless steel straw bottle.

### 2.1 The Critical Pediatric Amblyopia Window (Ages 4–7)
- Amblyopia ("lazy eye") occurs when the brain suppresses visual input from an misaligned or unequal eye.
- **The Strict Cortical Window:** If visual pathways are not properly aligned and stimulated before **age 7**, the neural wiring from that eye to the visual cortex is permanently lost, causing irreversible vision loss. Ensure routine pediatric instrumental vision photo-screening at the 4-year and 5-year well-child visits.

### 2.2 4-to-6 Year Immunization Boosters (Kindergarten Mandate)
At the 4-to-5-year pediatric visit, children receive essential booster doses:
1. **DTaP Booster:** Diphtheria, Tetanus, and acellular Pertussis (5th dose).
2. **IPV Booster:** Inactivated Poliovirus vaccine (4th dose).
3. **MMR Booster:** Measles, Mumps, and Rubella (2nd dose).
4. **Varicella Booster:** Chickenpox (2nd dose).
5. **Annual Influenza Vaccine.**

## 3. Daily Routine & Schedule Template (Preschool & Kindergarten)

- **6:45 AM – 7:15 AM:** Wake, morning cuddle, bathroom / wash face, get dressed.
- **7:15 AM – 7:45 AM:** **Breakfast** (scrambled eggs with spinach, whole grain toast, berries, water).
- **7:45 AM – 8:15 AM:** Backpack check, shoes on, 5-minute calm transition to school.
- **8:30 AM – 2:30 PM:** **School / Kindergarten Day** (social cooperative play, early literacy, outdoor recess, school lunch).
- **2:45 PM – 3:30 PM:** Afternoon arrival home: **The "After-School Decompression Sanctuary"**:
  - *Zero interrogation:* Do not pepper child with 20 questions ("What did you do? Who did you play with?").
  - Protein/fat snack (sliced apples with almond butter, cheese stick) + quiet sensory recovery.
- **3:30 PM – 5:30 PM:** Outdoor physical movement (biking, climbing, playground, nature walk).
- **6:00 PM – 6:45 PM:** **Family Dinner** (seated together at table, sharing highs and lows of the day).
- **7:00 PM – 7:45 PM:** Bedtime sequence (warm bath, teeth brushing with fluoridated paste, pajamas, reading 2–3 picture books aloud).
- **7:45 PM – 8:00 PM:** Somatic lullaby / back rub, lights out, deep 10–11 hour restorative sleep.
