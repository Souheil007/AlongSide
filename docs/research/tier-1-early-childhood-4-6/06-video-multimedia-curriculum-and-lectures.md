## Master Video 1: "The Power of Believing That You Can Improve (Growth Mindset)" — Dr. Carol Dweck

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hiiEeMN7vbQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hiiEeMN7vbQ)
- **Presenter:** Dr. Carol Dweck, PhD (Professor of Psychology, Stanford University; Author of *Mindset*)
- **Channel:** TED
- **Duration:** 10 minutes 24 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"When you tell your 5-year-old 'You are so smart!', you might think you are building their confidence. Neuroscience shows you are actually setting them up to give up the moment a task gets hard."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Trap of Fixed Praise:** Praising inherent intelligence ("You're a natural at puzzles!") creates a "Fixed Mindset": the child fears failure because making a mistake proves they are not smart.
2. **Praising the Process ("The Power of Yet"):** Praising effort, strategy, persistence, and focus ("You kept trying different puzzle pieces until you found the one that fit!") wires a "Growth Mindset": neural pathways fire and expand when confronting frustration.
3. **The Prefrontal Neuroplasticity Lesson:** Teaching young children that their brain is a muscle that grows stronger with effort transforms their relationship to school and errors.

### AlongSide Academy Course Integration:
- **Module:** *Academy Tier 1 — Lesson 2: Praising the Struggle, Not the Trophy*
- **Parent Habit Tracker:** "Replace 3 instances of 'Good boy / You're so smart' with process-based praise today."

## Master Video 2: "How Screens Stole Childhood — And How to Get It Back" — Jonathan Haidt

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH9L7vJ03DE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH9L7vJ03DE)
- **Presenter:** Jonathan Haidt, PhD (Social Psychologist, NYU Stern; Author of *The Anxious Generation*)
- **Channel:** TED
- **Duration:** 15 minutes 44 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"Why the shift from a 'play-based childhood' to a 'phone-based childhood' is the single greatest threat to adolescent mental health, and why the foundation must be defended between ages 4 and 6."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Great Rewiring of Childhood:** How the rapid adoption of touchscreen smartphones in the early 2010s displaced physical free play, rough-and-tumble games, and face-to-face conflict resolution.
2. **The Antidote in Early Childhood:** Why preschoolers need vast amounts of unsupervised physical play, climbing, dirt exploration, and neighborhood interaction to calibrate their vestibular balance and risk evaluation systems.
3. **The 4 Community Norms:** Establishing the foundation early: no personal smartphones before high school; no social media before 16; device-free schools; vast real-world independence.

### AlongSide Academy Course Integration:
- **Module:** *Academy Tier 1 — Lesson 4: Defending the Play-Based Childhood*
- **Outdoor Play Counter:** In-app tracker measuring daily outdoor movement hours vs. screen minutes.

## Master Video 3: "The Gardener vs. The Carpenter: How Children Learn" — Prof. Alison Gopnik

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MBqVIBwBgAc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MBqVIBwBgAc) *(Cross-referenced with Gopnik's Harvard & Authors@Google Lectures)*
- **Presenter:** Prof. Alison Gopnik (UC Berkeley)
- **Channel:** Talks at Google / UC Berkeley
- **Duration:** 42 minutes 15 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"Are you raising your child like a Carpenter (trying to carve raw material into a precise, high-achieving finished product) or like a Gardener (creating a rich, protected, diverse ecosystem where unpredictable beauty can flourish)?"*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Carpenter Fallacy:** Modern hyper-parenting treats children like lumber to be sanded, cut, and polished for elite resumes. This creates high anxiety and fragile perfectionism.
2. **The Gardener Paradigm:** The role of a parent is to provide rich soil, sturdy fences, and loving sunlight, allowing the child's unique developmental blueprint to unfold.
3. **Exploration vs. Exploitation in the Brain:** Why early childhood must remain optimized for broad exploration rather than narrow adult-like specialization.

### AlongSide Academy Course Integration:
- **Module:** *Academy Tier 1 — Lesson 6: The Gardener's Stance*
- **Reflective Exercise:** Identifying parental perfectionism and releasing unnecessary preschool academic drilling.
