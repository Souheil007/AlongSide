## 1. AAP Clinical Policy on Media for Preschool & Kindergarten (Ages 4–6)

The American Academy of Pediatrics framework for children ages 4 to 6 emphasizes quality, content curation, and strict time limits:

> **AAP Clinical Standard (Ages 4–6):**  
> **Limit screen time to 1 hour per day of high-quality, prosocial programming.**  
> Monitor media content and prioritize interactive, creative, and educational software over passive video consumption.  
> **Ensure media does not displace physical play, family conversation, and at least 10–11 hours of sleep.**

### 2.1 The Perils of YouTube Kids & Algorithmic Autoplay
- Many parents believe the "YouTube Kids" icon represents a safe digital nursery. In reality, algorithmic recommendation engines prioritize **watch time and eye-tracking capture**, surfacing bizarre, algorithmically generated content, garish unboxing videos, and repetitive sensory loops.
- **The Golden Rule:** Never allow open, unmonitored browsing inside video streaming platforms. Curate a strict, pre-downloaded offline playlist of designated shows.

### 2.2 Minecraft Creative Mode: Safe Introduction to Digital Construction
- If video gaming is introduced at age 5–6, **Minecraft in Creative Mode (Peaceful, Offline)** offers genuine spatial leverage:
  - Zero monsters, zero violence, zero death mechanics.
  - Acts as infinite digital LEGO blocks: child plans blueprints, selects materials, and builds architectural structures.
  - Must remain strictly capped at **30–45 minutes on weekend mornings**, never on school nights.

## 3. The 4 Non-Negotiable Digital Sanctuaries (Ages 4–6)

1. **The Device-Free Bedroom Sanctuary:** Tablets, smart TVs, and gaming consoles are strictly prohibited in children's bedrooms. A child's bedroom is a sensory sanctuary reserved exclusively for sleep, quiet physical reading, and imaginative play.
2. **The Device-Free Car Sanctuary:** Eliminate back-seat headrest DVD players and iPads for routine local car rides under 60 minutes. Looking out the car window, daydreaming, noticing street signs, and talking with parents in the front seat are crucial neurological foundations for spatial awareness and distress tolerance.
3. **The 60-Minute Pre-Bedtime Digital Curfew:** Screens shut down completely by **6:45 PM** to protect the natural 7:30 PM melatonin surge.
4. **Zero Screens During Mealtimes:** Neither adults nor children check devices at breakfast or dinner.

---

## 4. Preschool Digital Pact Clauses (AlongSide Family Agreement)

1. **The Shared Family Device Rule:** Tablets belong to the family and live on the kitchen charging dock, not in the child’s bedroom or personal backpack.
2. **The "Clean Transition" Commitment:** When the 45-minute timer rings, the child presses the pause button independently. If the child throws a violent meltdown, tablet privileges are automatically paused for 48 hours to allow dopamine receptors to reset.
3. **Parental Technoference Pledge:** Parents pledge to put smartphones in the charging basket during after-school reunion and dinner time (5:00 PM – 7:30 PM).
