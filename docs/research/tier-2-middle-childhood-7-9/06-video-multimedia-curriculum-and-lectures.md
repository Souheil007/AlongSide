## Master Video 1: "Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance" — Dr. Angela Duckworth

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H14bBuluwB8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H14bBuluwB8)
- **Presenter:** Dr. Angela Lee Duckworth, PhD (Professor of Psychology, University of Pennsylvania; Author of *Grit*)
- **Channel:** TED
- **Duration:** 6 minutes 12 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"Why natural talent and high IQ are surprisingly poor predictors of childhood success, and why learning to stay in the struggle when things get boring or difficult is the superpower of middle childhood."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Definition of Grit:** Perseverance and passion for long-term goals. Grit is holding onto your future day-in and day-out, not just for a week, but for years.
2. **The "Hard Thing Rule":** Every family member (including parents) picks one difficult activity that requires deliberate practice (e.g., piano, soccer, ballet, coding). You cannot quit in the middle of a season or on a bad day; you can only quit after the commitment ends.
3. **Talent Multiplied by Effort:** Skill is talent multiplied by effort; achievement is skill multiplied by effort. Effort counts twice.

- **Module:** *Academy Tier 2 — Lesson 3: Implementing the Hard Thing Rule*
- **Family Contract Module:** Interactive setup of the family "Hard Thing" ledger inside AlongSide.

## Master Video 2: "Growing Up in Public: Coming of Age in a Digital World" — Dr. Devorah Heitner

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kR66a-E1s4M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kR66a-E1s4M)
- **Presenter:** Dr. Devorah Heitner, PhD (Author of *Screenwise* and *Growing Up in Public*)
- **Channel:** Talks at Google / Parent Webinar Series
- **Duration:** 52 minutes 18 seconds (Key 7–9 Segment: 00:10:00–00:25:00)
- **The Parent Hook:** *"When your 8-year-old says 'Everyone has a phone, I need one!', how do you shift from digital police officer to trusted digital mentor without isolating them from peers?"*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **Mentorship Over Monitoring:** Spyware and keyloggers do not teach children digital wisdom; they merely teach children how to hide things more covertly. Mentorship means co-viewing, open communication, and transparent boundaries.
2. **The Group Chat Precursor:** Why elementary school smartwatches should be limited strictly to family and close friends, preventing chaotic group drama before children have emotional maturity.
3. **The "Sharenting" Reckoning:** How parental posting of children's photos and embarrassing moments violates child trust and creates digital footprints before consent is possible.

## Master Video 3: "Are Smartphones Ruining Childhood?" — Jonathan Haidt

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KNEGWrD08f8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KNEGWrD08f8)
- **Presenter:** Jonathan Haidt, PhD (NYU Stern)
- **Channel:** TED Talks Daily / Conversations
- **Duration:** 24 minutes 35 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"A devastating look at the epidemiological data on adolescent mental health, and why defending ages 7 to 9 from premature smartphone introduction is the single highest-ROI parenting decision you will ever make."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Collective Action Trap:** Parents cave in to smartphones not because they think smartphones are good, but because they fear their child will be socially excluded ("The Prisoner's Dilemma of Parenting").
2. **Community Pacts:** How parents can band together (e.g., "Wait Until 8th" pledge) so multiple families in the same 3rd-grade class agree to delay smartphones simultaneously.
3. **Reclaiming Unsupervised Play:** Re-establishing free-range childhood: letting 8-year-olds walk around the block, ride bikes with friends, and resolve their own playground disputes.
