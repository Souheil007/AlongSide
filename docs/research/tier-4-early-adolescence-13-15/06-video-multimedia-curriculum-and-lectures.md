## Master Video 1: "How Do Teenagers Sleep Differently?" — Dr. Matthew Walker

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oDRrRuPqALs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oDRrRuPqALs)
- **Presenter:** Dr. Matthew Walker, PhD (Professor of Neuroscience and Psychology, UC Berkeley; Author of *Why We Sleep*)
- **Channel:** Matthew Walker / Talks
- **Duration:** 6 minutes 48 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"You think your 14-year-old is staying up until 11:30 PM playing on their phone because they are lazy or defiant. In reality, their brain has undergone an evolutionary 2-hour circadian shift that makes falling asleep at 10 PM biologically impossible."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Biological Phase Delay:** Why pubertal melatonin secretion begins 2 hours later than in adults, shifting the natural sleep gate to 11:00 PM – 11:30 PM.
2. **The School Start Time Catastrophe:** How 7:30 AM high school start times amputate REM sleep (the critical sleep stage where emotional memories are integrated and psychiatric stability is restored).
3. **The Prefrontal Shield:** Why sleep deprivation impairs adolescent prefrontal executive function, mimicking ADHD, bipolar swings, and depression.

### AlongSide Academy Course Integration:
- **Module:** *Academy Tier 4 — Lesson 2: The Circadian Melatonin Shift*
- **Parent Action:** "Adjust bedtime expectations: shift focus from forcing sleep at 9:30 PM to establishing a wind-down dim-light routine with screens off by 9:00 PM."

## Master Video 2: "The Teenage Brain: A Neuroscientist's Survival Guide" — Dr. Frances Jensen

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=epLmx8xW-QU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=epLmx8xW-QU)
- **Presenter:** Dr. Frances E. Jensen, MD, FACP (Chair of Neurology, Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania; Author of *The Teenage Brain*)
- **Channel:** Talks at Google / Harvard Medical School
- **Duration:** 54 minutes 30 seconds (Key 13–15 Segment: 00:08:00–00:28:00)
- **The Parent Hook:** *"Your teenager has a brain that is 80% wired. Discover why the last 20%—the frontal lobe cables—explains everything from sudden risk-taking to heightened vulnerability to addiction."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **Back-to-Front Myelination:** The physical sequence of oligodendrocyte insulation; why impulse control is the last cognitive function to wire.
2. **High Synaptic Plasticity & Addiction:** Why nicotine, alcohol, and algorithmic social media wire habit loops in teenagers in half the time it takes in adults.
3. **The Scaffolded Prefrontal Cortex:** How parents must serve as their teenager's external prefrontal cortex without suffocating their growing independence.

### AlongSide Academy Course Integration:
- **Module:** *Academy Tier 4 — Lesson 4: The 80% Wired Brain*
- **Family Conversation Guide:** Sharing the "Back-to-Front" brain concept with your teen so they understand their own biology.

## Master Video 3: "Age of Opportunity: Lessons From the New Science of Adolescence" — Dr. Laurence Steinberg

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_lT3yJ7h5g](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_lT3yJ7h5g)
- **Presenter:** Dr. Laurence Steinberg, PhD (Distinguished Professor of Psychology, Temple University)
- **Channel:** Woodrow Wilson School / NYSAIS Lectures
- **Duration:** 1 hour 02 minutes (Key Risk Segment: 00:14:00–00:35:00)
- **The Parent Hook:** *"Watch the laboratory experiment that proves having just two peers in the room doubles a teenager's risk of crashing a car, and what parents can do to protect their teens during high-arousal social situations."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Dual-Systems Model:** The gap between the early pubertal maturation of sensation-seeking and the protracted maturation of cognitive control.
2. **The Social Multiplier on Sensation:** How peer observation activates the ventral striatum's dopamine circuits, shutting down prefrontal caution.
3. **The Importance of Warmth and Sturdy Firmness (Authoritative Parenting):** Combining high parental warmth with firm, consistent, non-punitive guardrails produces the most resilient, high-achieving adolescents.

### AlongSide Academy Course Integration:
- **Module:** *Academy Tier 4 — Lesson 6: The Peer Multiplier Effect*
- **Safety Tool:** Setting up the "Emergency Car Ride Pact": zero questions asked when calling for a safe ride home from a party.
