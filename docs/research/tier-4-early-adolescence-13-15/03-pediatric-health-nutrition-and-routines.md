## 1. Pediatric Nutrition Science & Adolescent Caloric Demands

### 1.1 The Voracious Caloric Surge
During the pubertal peak height velocity window, adolescent metabolic rates explode:
- **Caloric Requirements:** An active 14-year-old athlete requires **2,500 to 3,500 kcal per day** to sustain tissue remodeling, brain development, and bone mineralization.
- **Protein Synthesis:** 1.2 to 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day (eggs, Greek yogurt, grass-fed meats, wild fish, lentils, hemp hearts) to support rapid myofibrillar hypertrophy.
- **The Chronic Dehydration Threat:** Adolescents frequently confuse thirst with fatigue or boredom, consuming caffeinated energy drinks that exacerbate dehydration and sleep disruption. Target: **64 to 80 ounces (2.0–2.5 L) of clean water daily**.

### 2.1 The 6-Step Gradual Return-to-Play Concussion Protocol
If a teenager suffers a direct or indirect blow to the head during sports:
1. **Step 1: Symptom-Limited Physical and Cognitive Rest (24–48 hours):** Zero screens, zero schoolwork, dark quiet room, plenty of sleep.
2. **Step 2: Light Aerobic Exercise:** 15 minutes of stationary cycling or light walking (HR <70% max; no resistance training).
3. **Step 3: Sport-Specific Exercise:** Running drills, agility movement; zero head impact.
4. **Step 4: Non-Contact Training Drills:** Passing drills, complex tactical coordination, light weight training.
5. **Step 5: Full-Contact Practice:** Only after medical clearance by a licensed physician.
6. **Step 6: Full Return to Competitive Play.**
*Rule: If any symptom recurs at any step, the athlete must drop back to the previous asymptomatic stage for 24 hours.*

### 2.2 Acne Vulgaris Management (Mental Health Protection)
Adolescent acne is not merely cosmetic; it directly correlates with clinical depression and social anxiety:
- First-line medical therapy: OTC topical retinoid (Adapalene 0.1% gel) applied nightly with a pea-sized amount, paired with Benzoyl Peroxide 2.5–5% wash in the morning.
- For nodulocystic scarring acne, refer promptly to dermatology for oral isotretinoin (Accutane) evaluation before permanent physical scarring occurs.

## 3. Daily Routine & Schedule Template (High School: Ages 13–15)

- **6:15 AM – 6:45 AM:** Wake, morning light exposure (open blinds immediately to reset suprachiasmatic nucleus), quick shower, acne wash, deodorant, dress.
- **6:45 AM – 7:10 AM:** **High-Protein Breakfast** (3 scrambled eggs with spinach and cheese, whole grain bagel, tall glass of water with electrolytes).
- **7:20 AM – 2:45 PM:** **High School Academic Day** (rigorous academic schedule, extracurriculars).
- **3:00 PM – 5:00 PM:** Athletics / Sports Practice / Drama / Music rehearsal.
- **5:15 PM – 6:00 PM:** **Afternoon Decompression & Healthy Fuel:**
  - Protein smoothie (banana, whey/pea protein, peanut butter, berries).
  - 30 minutes of unstructured downtime.
- **6:00 PM – 7:30 PM:** **Focused Academic Homework Sprint:**
  - Smartphone stays in the kitchen charging dock, completely out of the study room.
  - Pomodoro sprints (45 minutes study, 10 minutes physical stretch).
- **7:30 PM – 8:15 PM:** **Family Dinner** (sharing conversational dialogue, no screens allowed at table).
- **8:30 PM: THE DIGITAL LOCKDOWN CURFEW (AlongSide Pact-as-Code):**
  - Entertainment apps, social media, and algorithmic streaming lock automatically via OS profile.
- **8:45 PM – 9:45 PM:** Creative leisure, reading physical books, talking with family, listening to vinyl/music.
- **9:45 PM – 10:15 PM:** Nighttime wind-down (warm shower, dim yellow lighting, journaling).
- **10:15 PM – 10:30 PM:** Lights out, room cool (66–68°F), achieving 8.5–9 hours of deep restorative sleep.
