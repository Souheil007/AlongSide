### 1.1 The Anatomy of Financial Sextortion (FBI / NCMEC Warning)
The FBI and NCMEC report an explosive surge in organized international financial sextortion schemes targeting adolescent boys ages 13 to 17:
1. **The Honeytrap Hook:** A predator posing as a teenage girl contacts the victim on Instagram or Snapchat, engaging in rapid flirtatious messaging.
2. **The Escalation:** The predator sends an attractive, stolen explicit image and demands an explicit photo in return.
3. **The Immediate Blackmail Strike:** Within 60 seconds of receiving the image, the predator reveals their true identity, sends screenshots of the teen's Instagram followers list (family, friends, soccer teammates), and demands $500–$2,000 via Apple Gift Cards, CashApp, or crypto: *"Pay right now or this photo is being sent to your mother, your principal, and everyone on your team."*
4. **The Catastrophic Acute Phase:** The teen enters overwhelming autonomic panic, believing their life and reputation are permanently ruined. Several tragic adolescent suicides have occurred within hours of this threat.

## 3. High School Pact-as-Code Architecture (Apple `.mobileconfig` / Android Policy)

AlongSide’s cryptographic profile compiler generates OS-level enforcement without invasive surveillance:

1. **The 9:00 PM Bedtime Lockout Profile:**
   - At **9:00 PM on school nights (Sunday–Thursday)**, all social media (Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat), gaming, and video streaming apps automatically lock at the operating system kernel level.
   - The device functions strictly as an essential communication tool: phone calls, family messaging, calculator, notes, and music remain active.
2. **The 24/7 Lifeline Sanctuary (Zero Lockout for Emergency Services):**
   - The phone dialer, emergency 911, and the **National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (988)** are cryptographically whitelisted and can never be disabled by parental controls or bedtime schedules.
3. **The Zero-Spyware Commitment:**
   - AlongSide does NOT read personal texts, log keystrokes, or intercept direct messages. Respecting teen privacy preserves the parent-child attachment bond, guaranteeing the teen will come forward when real emergencies occur.

## 4. High School Digital Pact Clauses (AlongSide Agreement Engine)

1. **The Central Charging Docking Station:** Devices are plugged into the kitchen charging station by **9:00 PM on school nights and 10:30 PM on weekends**. Devices never spend the night in the teenager's bedroom.
2. **The Study Desk Sanctuary:** During academic study hours (5:30 PM – 7:30 PM), smartphones remain out of the room to prevent constant dopamine interruptions from group chats.
3. **The Driving Safety Covenant:** When the teen begins driver’s education, zero texting or handheld browsing while behind the wheel. The phone goes into "Do Not Disturb While Driving" mode automatically.
