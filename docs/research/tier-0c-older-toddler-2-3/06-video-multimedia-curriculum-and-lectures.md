## Master Video 1: "Potty Training Without Bribes, Fear, or Pressure" — Dr. Becky Kennedy

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qi4VwpEs3LE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qi4VwpEs3LE) *(Cross-referenced with Dr. Becky's Good Inside Potty Learning Masterclass)*
- **Presenter:** Dr. Becky Kennedy, PhD (Clinical Psychologist, Founder of Good Inside)
- **Channel:** Good Inside / Huberman Lab Discussions
- **Duration:** 28 minutes 40 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"Sticker charts, M&Ms, and potty bribes seem like they work—until your child withholds their poop for four days and ends up in agonizing tears. Learn why true potty learning requires autonomy, not external bribery."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Sovereignty of Elimination:** Eating, sleeping, and toileting are the three things a parent cannot physically force a child to do. Attempting to control them creates catastrophic power struggles.
2. **Why Bribes Backfire in Potty Training:** Offering treats for peeing in the toilet shifts the motivation from internal bodily awareness to external reward, increasing performance anxiety and triggering voluntary withholding.
3. **The Non-Pressured Language Protocol:** Shifting from *"Go try to pee on the potty right now!"* to *"Your body knows how to listen to pee and poop. You're the boss of your body."*

- **Module:** *Academy Tier 0C — Lesson 3: The Peaceful Potty Transition*

## Master Video 2: "Toddler Tantrums: What Is Actually Happening in the Brain?" — Dr. Laura Markham

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XT_6Lvkhxvo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XT_6Lvkhxvo) *(Aha! Parenting Clinical Masterclass Series)*
- **Presenter:** Dr. Laura Markham, PhD (Clinical Psychologist; Author of *Peaceful Parent, Happy Kids*)
- **Channel:** Aha! Parenting
- **Duration:** 15 minutes 10 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"When your 2-year-old throws themselves onto the supermarket floor screaming because you wouldn't buy the candy bar, what is happening inside their neural circuits, and how does your nervous system either cure or inflame the storm?"*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Amygdala Hijack in Toddlers:** An older toddler's amygdala triggers an emergency survival response to trivial boundaries because their emotional braking system has zero myelination.
2. **The Mirror Neuron Resonance:** When a parent yells, tenses their shoulders, or rolls their eyes, the child’s mirror neurons absorb that threat cue, prolonging the tantrum by 15–20 minutes.
3. **The "Empathy Sandwich":** Validating the feeling, holding the immovable boundary, and offering loving presence.

## Master Video 3: "How Children Develop Self-Control (Executive Function)" — Harvard Center on the Developing Child

- **Platform / URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s31WJj_3vEE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s31WJj_3vEE)
- **Presenter:** Dr. Jack P. Shonkoff, MD (Director, Center on the Developing Child at Harvard University)
- **Channel:** Harvard University Center on the Developing Child
- **Duration:** 5 minutes 18 seconds
- **The Parent Hook:** *"Executive function and self-regulation are like an air traffic control system for the brain. See how pretend play, turn-taking, and predictable routines lay the runway in older toddlers."*

### Key Learning Points & Clinical Concepts:
1. **The Air Traffic Control Metaphor:** Toddlers have dozens of sensory inputs and impulses landing simultaneously; their developing prefrontal cortex needs scaffolding to prioritize and sequence.
2. **The Three Core Dimensions:** Working memory, inhibitory control, and mental flexibility.
3. **Scaffolding Through Play:** How games like Freeze Dance and imaginary cooking build neurological pathways for adult resilience and academic focus.
